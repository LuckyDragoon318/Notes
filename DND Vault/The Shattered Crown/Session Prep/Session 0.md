Character Creation, D&D intro
Introduce the PHB
   The Players Handbook is the core of D&D, it is the only requirement to play the game
Character sheets
   **Class**
      The core of your character. Your class comes with perks at every level
   **Species**
      Your characters species, each species has perks special to itself
   **Background**
      This is what your character did before the game. You take this one word background and use it to write your backstory, or you can do the opposite. Each one comes with special perks
   **Feats, Traits**
      Your character can gain certain special traits depending on your class, background, and species. Your character sheet has these organized into Class Features, Species Traits, and Feats. Class features, and Species Traits, like the name implies come from your class and species. Feats are more complicated and usually come in place of another benefit when your character reaches certain levels. Your background can also give you some of these
      You should write down all of your class features in the two boxes on your character sheet, and add a very short description of what each one is. Do the same for your Species Traits
   **Ability Scores**
      Each character has 6 ability scores. Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Charisma, and Constitution. Each of these has 2 numbers tied to it that dictate what your character is good at, or what your character is exceptionally bad at
   **Spells?**
      Do you really want spells?
   **Appearance**
      This is what your character looks like, it can be anything you want, within the bounds of some constraint for each species. There are always exceptions though!
   **Backstory**
      A backstory is a huge part of D&D. Making a backstory for your character is a way for your dungeon master to include your character and their family into the [[Campaigns]], and helps you roleplay your character better
