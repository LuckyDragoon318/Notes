#INCOMPLETE
Ideally the players journey towards [[Lord Varek]]'s castle, otherwise they get kidnapped. 
If kidnapping is needed, a hooded figure offers assistance, or characters can make a DC 14 sleight of hand check to pick the lock on the transport. If players take the assistance, the hooded figure will get the guards attention and start a fight. Players can also start the fight themselves. Guards use the same stat block as the guards from [[session 1]].

The Hollow Wood
Forest from long ago, with but one building, an old house, with a strangely large basement
Basement is normal dungeon crawl style

Ambush!

Kings guard guards ambush players on the road. After combat, the first player to loot a guard will find a map of [[Lord Varek]]’s stronghold - not the castle, the stronghold - The map is accompanied by a coded letter

It's a simple code, DC 8 intelligence or wisdom to decode. It says that construction on the stronghold is nearly complete, and that the shards of the [[Crown of Domination]] are set to be delivered in 6 weeks.
